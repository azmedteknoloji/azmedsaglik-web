Mobile Apps

Apps that make everyday health tracking easier and work without an internet connection.

Adet Takvimi

Period Tracker

Cycle, symptom and fertility tracking

Period Tracker lets you follow your menstrual cycle, daily symptoms and fertile days in a single calendar. It needs no account and works completely offline; your records are stored encrypted on your device.

  • Cycle calendar with period, fertile window and ovulation-day predictions
  • Daily log: bleeding intensity, pain level, symptoms and basal body temperature
  • A cycle and symptom report you can show your doctor
  • PIN or biometric lock; available in Turkish and English

İlaç Hatırlatıcı

Medication Reminder

Big text, offline reminders

A reminder app for anyone who wants to take their medication on time, designed with older users in mind. No internet, no account, and nothing ever leaves your phone.

  • Recognise each medicine by a photo of its box
  • Alarms that are hard to miss, repeating after 5, 10 and 15 minutes
  • Very large text, simple mode and screen reader support
  • A one-page PDF report for your doctor; free, with no ads

Health Education Games

Informative games that explain important health topics through short, calm and instructive play.

Pusula

Fertility facts, as a game

A calm knowledge voyage for couples trying to conceive. Your boat sails an archipelago: every island is a topic and every stop is its own small game. Each stop you finish adds its knowledge cards to your Knowledge Treasure chest.

  • 7 islands, 7 topics: the male factor, PMOS and endometriosis, age and egg reserve, insemination, the IVF journey and more
  • 32 mini-games; myths are clearly marked, with the facts told right beside them
  • Doctor’s Notebook: the questions you want to ask at your appointment, in large type
  • No scores, no penalties; fully offline, no account and no ads

The medical content has been reviewed and approved by Assoc. Prof. Dr. Ayşe Zehra Özdemir Kaya.

Dokuz Ay

From dusk to sunrise, by your side for nine months.

A pregnancy guide that explains what to expect week by week through short, gentle games. Each month a new moon rises in the sky, and its training station holds games made for that stage of pregnancy.

  • 9 months, 17 short games: from nutrition and check-ups to breathing techniques and the hospital bag
  • Kick Counter: count your baby’s movements and review past counts
  • Emergency Signs: one tap away on every screen, always free
  • Baby Journal: week-by-week development; your data stays on your device

The medical content has been reviewed and approved by Assoc. Prof. Dr. Ayşe Zehra Özdemir Kaya.
