Our Products About Us

Who We Are

AZMED Sağlık Hizmetleri Ltd. is a health technology company that brings medical expertise together with modern software. We build digital solutions that make reliable health information easy to reach, while keeping personal data safe. Every product we make is grounded in physicians’ knowledge, scientific sources and the real needs of the people who use it.

What We Do

We create digital solutions across different areas of health, each shaped around a real need.

Mobile Health Apps

Simple mobile apps that make everyday health tracking easier and are comfortable for everyone to use.

AI-Supported Health Solutions

We are working on science-based, next-generation approaches that help make sense of health data.

Game-Based Health Education

We make complex health topics easier to understand through short, calm and instructive games.

Research & Development

We carry out our R&D activities at Ondokuz Mayıs University Samsun Technopark.

Our Approach

Every product we build follows the same four principles.

  • Privacy First

    Our apps are designed to work without an account; your data stays on your device.

  • Physician-Approved Content

    Our medical content is reviewed and approved by specialist physicians.

  • User-Centred Design

    Large text, high contrast and screen reader support make our interfaces accessible to everyone.

  • Scientific Foundation

    Our content rests on current medical knowledge and reliable sources, with no exaggerated promises.
