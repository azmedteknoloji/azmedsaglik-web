About Us

By bringing medical expertise together with technology, we make health knowledge reach more people, more safely.

Our Story

AZMED Sağlık Hizmetleri Limited Şirketi was founded on the idea of bringing medical expertise together with software development. We believe that the questions physicians answer every day can reach far more people with the right tools.

With that belief, we develop mobile apps and educational games in women’s health, reproductive health and everyday health tracking. The medical content of our products is reviewed by physicians, and privacy and accessibility always come first in our designs.

Our Mission

To bring scientifically accurate, physician-approved health information to everyone through digital products that respect privacy and are accessible and easy to use.

Our Vision

To build a trusted digital health ecosystem that brings individuals and healthcare professionals together, with women’s and reproductive health at its core.

Our Values

Reliability

We ground every piece of content in scientific sources and have it approved by physicians.

Privacy

We process personal health data only where necessary, in line with applicable regulations, and treat its protection as a priority.

Accessibility

We design products that everyone can use comfortably, whatever their age or experience.

Responsibility

We state clearly that our products do not replace a doctor’s examination.

Continuous Improvement

We keep improving our products through user feedback and current scientific developments.

People First

We value technology to the extent that it answers people’s real needs.

R&D at Samsun Teknopark

At the Samsun Technology Development Zone (Samsun Teknopark), we are continuing our R&D work on AZMed+, a project that takes a holistic approach to women’s and reproductive health. By combining medical expertise with artificial intelligence, we aim to develop next-generation digital solutions for individuals and healthcare professionals.
